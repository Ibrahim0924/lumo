What is included for free?

You can create up to 3 decks with up to 10 cards each and study them in Flashcards mode. Lumo Premium removes these limits and adds Quiz and Write modes, card import, Streak Freeze, weekly activity and extra deck colors.

I bought Premium but it is not active

Open Settings in the app, tap Upgrade to Premium, then tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Study Flashcards: Lumo and tap Cancel Subscription. Premium stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.

Where are my decks stored?

Your decks, cards and progress are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.
