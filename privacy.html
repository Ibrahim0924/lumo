Privacy Policy

Study Flashcards: Lumo · Last updated November 22, 2026

Study Flashcards: Lumo (“Lumo”, “the app”) is published by DEDUSHKA DEVELOPMENT - FZCO. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Lumo does not collect your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Lumo has no accounts and no sign-up. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not send your content to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The decks and cards you create, your study progress, your streak and your app settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see or access them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your Apple Account settings.

Purchases

Lumo Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number or billing address. The app only checks with Apple whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account, so it can unlock Premium features. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Lumo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
