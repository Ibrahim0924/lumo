Privacy Policy

Study Flashcards: Lumo · Last updated November 22, 2026

Study Flashcards: Lumo (“Lumo”, “the app”) is published by DEDUSHKA DEVELOPMENT - FZCO. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Lumo has no accounts, shows no ads, does not track you, and keeps your decks on your device. We only receive anonymous statistics about installs and subscriptions.

Information we collect

Lumo has no accounts and no sign-up, so we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. The app contains no advertising, and it does not send your decks or cards to us or to anyone else.

To see how many people install the app and how subscriptions perform, Lumo uses Adapty, a subscription analytics service that processes data on our behalf. Through Adapty we receive:

  • a random identifier created for your installation of the app, and the identifier your device assigns to our apps (IDFV);
  • your Lumo Premium purchase history: the plan, and when it started, renewed, was canceled or expired;
  • when the app is opened;
  • general device details: model, iOS version, language, time zone, App Store country, and the country your internet connection comes from.

This information is not linked to your name or contact details, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We have turned off collection of the advertising identifier. Adapty’s handling of this data is described in the Adapty privacy policy for end users.

Information stored on your device

The decks and cards you create, your study progress, your streak and your app settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see or access them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your Apple Account settings.

Purchases

Lumo Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, card number or billing address. The app checks with Apple whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account, so it can unlock Premium features, and reports the subscription to Adapty as described above. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Lumo does not ask anyone, including children, for a name, contact details or any other information that identifies them personally.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
